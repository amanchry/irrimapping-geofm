    Progress report¶

    Where the research stands against the research plan and the research proposal: what is finished, what came out, what is next and what is still open. Every individual change is in the change log.

    Last updated: 6 November 2026

    Summary¶

    The dataset is complete: 8,781 field-surveyed parcels with Sentinel-1/2 time series of their agricultural year, five spatial folds and a shared tile store. Embeddings of all four foundation models are computed. The first RQ1 experiment shows that no frozen foundation model beats a well-designed Sentinel-1/2 baseline on average (macro-F1 0.910 for the best pair vs 0.906), while Clay and Prithvi hold up better in the hardest region. The main RQ1 run with the final validation design is ready, and a second way of combining the months (L-TAE) is planned. The experiment setup, documentation and website are in place, so every result can be rerun and traced.

    Key results¶

    Experiment Result
    E01 · frozen GeoFMs + PCA, nested CV No GeoFM beats the S1/S2 baseline after correcting for multiple tests (best Clay + SVM 0.910 vs baseline + SVM 0.906). Linear probe: TerraMind and Clay equal the baseline. AgriFM is behind. In the hardest fold (rainfed in Doukkala and Souss) the baseline drops to 0.80 while Clay and Prithvi stay higher.

    Next steps¶

    1. Run E02 (PASTIS validation): the main RQ1 result.
    2. Confirm AgriFM's input normalisation; re-embed if needed.
    3. Write the L-TAE pipeline (models_run/03c_train_ltae.py) and run E03.
    4. Results per region and per season from the out-of-fold predictions.
    5. Start RQ2: segmentation head (UPerNet) and LoRA fine-tuning; neural baselines TempCNN and U-TAE.

    Open points and risks¶

    Point Why it matters Next action
    AgriFM pixel scale (its config mean ~4,180 vs ~1,100 in Earth Engine Sentinel-2) AgriFM may be under-rated in E01 check the scale its weights expect
    Prithvi pretrained on 30 m HLS, used at 10 m possible handicap state it; optional 30 m test
    Neighbouring fields inside a chip mixed patches for small parcels accuracy by parcel size; optional masked-chip test
    Region and year partly confounded a "hard region" may be a "hard year" per-year results; year test within Doukkala / El Haouz
    Transfer region (RQ4) Tunisia has only 10 rainfed reference points decide between Tunisia and US dryland regions after RQ1–RQ2
    Wall-to-wall mapping (RQ3) parcel pooling needs polygons segmentation head and pixel models (D11)
