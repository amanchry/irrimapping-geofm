    Foundation models¶

    Four geospatial foundation models (GeoFMs) are evaluated in this study. It summarises their design and pretraining, specifies how each model is applied to the Moroccan parcels, and lists the primary sources. Other pages refer to this page rather than repeating its content.

    Selection¶

    The four models were selected according to three criteria: (i) pretrained weights are openly available and can be fine-tuned; (ii) the models accept multi-temporal Sentinel-1 and/or Sentinel-2 input; and (iii) together they represent the principal GeoFM families, namely masked-autoencoder Vision Transformers (Prithvi-EO-2.0, Clay), a multimodal generative model (TerraMind) and an agriculture-specific spatio-temporal model (AgriFM).

    Overview¶

    Model Developer Architecture Pretraining data Paper Weights / code
    Clay v1.5 Clay Foundation Vision Transformer, masked autoencoder with DINO distillation; sensor-agnostic band encoding ~70 million chips from several sensors (Sentinel-2, Landsat, Sentinel-1, NAIP, among others) Clay Foundation (2024) Hugging Face · code
    Prithvi-EO-2.0-300M-TL NASA, IBM multi-temporal Vision Transformer (masked autoencoder) with temporal and location embeddings 4.2 million Harmonised Landsat-Sentinel-2 (HLS) time series, 30 m Szwarcman et al. (2024) Hugging Face
    TerraMind-1.0-base IBM, ESA any-to-any generative multimodal transformer 9 million spatio-temporally aligned samples of nine modalities, including Sentinel-1, Sentinel-2 and a DEM Jakubik et al. (2025) Hugging Face
    AgriFM Li et al. (2025) modified Video Swin Transformer for agricultural mapping ~25 million samples of MODIS, Landsat and Sentinel-2 time series Li et al. (2025) code · weights

    Configuration in this study¶

    All encoders are used frozen, i.e. as fixed feature extractors without gradient updates. Each model receives a 64 × 64 px chip (640 m) centred on the parcel, with the bands and normalisation statistics of its own pretraining. The parcel embedding is the mean of the patch tokens covering the parcel, weighted by the fraction of each patch inside the parcel (Models pipeline §5). The Sentinel-2 feature baseline is listed for comparison. Every input uses Sentinel-2 only (D14).

    Input Chips Bands Patch Temporal handling Output per parcel Script
    S2 baseline parcel features (inner pixels) 12 S2 bands, NDVI, NDWI, EVI parcel median 12 months concatenated 180 features 03
    Clay v1.5 p8 10 S2 bands 8 px (80 m) one month per forward pass 12 × 1024 02a
    Prithvi-EO-2.0 p16 6 S2 bands (HLS set) 16 px (160 m) four months per pass, three passes 12 × 1024 02b
    TerraMind p16 12 S2 bands 16 px (160 m) one month per forward pass 12 × 768 02c
    AgriFM p16 10 S2 bands 4 px pretrained patch embedding; output stride 32 px (320 m) full 12-month stack in one pass 1 × 1024 02d

    Sentinel-1 is left out of the main comparison (D14): every model then sees the same optical data, and Sentinel-1 coverage of Utah changed between the years. An optional ablation (Morocco only) adds it back where a model was pretrained on it: TerraMind with Sentinel-1 (MODALITIES = ["S2L2A", "S1GRD"] in script 02c, written as terramind-s1s2_agyear_tiles.npz) and the baseline with radar features (S1S2_VARIABLES = "all" in script 03).

    Model notes¶

    Clay v1.5¶

    Clay encodes each band together with its central wavelength, which allows a single model to process imagery from different sensors. Ten Sentinel-2 bands (B2–B8A, B11, B12) are standardised with the statistics of Clay's metadata.yaml. Additional inputs are the pixel size (10 m), the acquisition date (week of year, sine/cosine encoded; monthly composites are dated mid-month) and the location (latitude and longitude, sine/cosine encoded). The model processes one date per forward pass, so the twelve months are embedded independently. With 8 px patches, a chip yields an 8 × 8 token grid.

    Prithvi-EO-2.0-300M-TL¶

    Prithvi-EO-2.0 is pretrained on HLS time series and encodes acquisition date and location explicitly. The six HLS bands (Blue, Green, Red, Narrow NIR, SWIR 1, SWIR 2; Sentinel-2 B2, B3, B4, B8A, B11, B12) are standardised with the model's pretraining statistics. Following the pretraining configuration, four frames are processed per pass (September–December, January–April, May–August); each frame retains its own tokens, giving one vector per month. The model is loaded through TerraTorch (prithvi_eo_v2_300_tl). Limitation: pretraining used 30 m HLS data, whereas 10 m Sentinel-2 is used here; the resulting 160 m patch is closer to typical parcel size than the 480 m patch at native resolution.

    TerraMind-1.0-base¶

    TerraMind is trained to generate any of nine modalities from any other and therefore learns a joint representation of optical, radar and auxiliary data. Two modalities are supplied: S2L2A (B1–B12) and S1GRD (VV, VH in dB), each standardised with TerraMind's pretraining statistics. The tokens of the two modalities are averaged (merge_method = "mean"). One date is processed per pass, and 16 px patches give a 4 × 4 token grid.

    AgriFM¶

    AgriFM is designed for crop mapping and models the temporal dimension within the network. The full 12-month stack of ten Sentinel-2 bands is processed in a single pass. Input tokens span four time steps and 4 × 4 px (the pretrained patch embedding); the final feature map has a stride of 32 px (D12). Because time is compressed inside the network, the output is one vector per parcel and season. Only the encoder is used, through the mmengine registries of the cloned repository.

    Normalisation (D15). Neither the AgriFM paper nor the repository documents the normalisation of the pretraining data; the only published statistics belong to the repository's fine-tuning example (configs/cropland_mapping.py, blue 4,179 ± 4,042). The cloud-masked composites of this study are on a different scale (Morocco blue 714 ± 344), so with the config statistics the visible and NIR bands would use only 8–30% of the expected spread. AgriFM is therefore normalised per band with the mean and standard deviation of the region's own chips (NORM = "data"; statistics saved to agrifm_norm_stats_<tag>.json), the usual choice when the pretraining statistics are unknown. The config statistics are kept as a sensitivity run (NORM = "config", file agrifm-cfgnorm_*.npz).

    Weights¶

    All weights are stored in checkpoints/<model>/ (excluded from version control) and are downloaded automatically on the first run of the corresponding embedding script.

    Model Folder Downloaded by
    Clay v1.5 checkpoints/clay/ (clay-v1.5.ckpt, metadata.yaml) models_run/02a_embed_clay.py
    Prithvi-EO-2.0-300M-TL checkpoints/prithvi/ (Hugging Face cache) models_run/02b_embed_prithvi.py
    TerraMind-1.0-base checkpoints/terramind/ (Hugging Face cache) models_run/02c_embed_terramind.py
    AgriFM checkpoints/agrifm/ (AgriFM.pth); code in external/AgriFM models_run/02d_embed_agrifm.py
