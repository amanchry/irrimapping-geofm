==============================================================================

Files

Made by s2_download/01_s2_grid_overview.py from the 2023/24 cropland mask (cropland_extent/make_cropland_mask.py).


1. Three sizes of square
------------------------
Three different squares appear in the figures. They are nested: the large ones contain the small ones.

  Sentinel-2 tile   109.8 km (10,980 px)  How ESA publishes the images: one file per tile, date and band
                                          (MGRS grid, e.g. 29SNR). Neighbouring tiles overlap by 9.8 km.
  Block             10.24 km (1,024 px)   Our download unit: one job reads every scene of the year for this
                                          square and builds the 12 monthly composites.
  Model tile        1.28 km (128 px)      What is stored, read by the models and predicted. Same tile_id and
                                          pixels as the cropland mask and the training tiles (03_build_tiles.py).

  1 block = 8 x 8 model tiles. A Sentinel-2 tile holds about 10 x 10 blocks.
  Blocks and model tiles follow our own grid (UTM of the region, 10 m, origin at 0/0); they are not cut from
  the Sentinel-2 tiles but read from them.


2. The three panels (left to right = zooming in)
------------------------------------------------
Left: the whole region
  Blue outlines: the Sentinel-2 tiles that touch at least one cropland block, with their MGRS code.
  Coloured squares: the 10.24 km blocks that contain cropland. The colour is the number of model tiles in the
  block with at least 1% cropland (0 to 64): dark = the block is almost entirely cropland, light = only a few
  of its tiles have cropland.
  Red outline: the Sentinel-2 tile shown in the middle panel.

Middle: one Sentinel-2 tile
  The same blocks, zoomed to one Sentinel-2 tile (red). Black square: the block shown in the right panel.
  Blocks near the edge of the red square lie in the overlap with the neighbouring Sentinel-2 tile.

Right: one block
  Green: cropland in the mask; light: not cropland.
  The 8 x 8 grid: the model tiles. Red squares are kept (at least 1% = 164 px of cropland) and downloaded;
  dotted grey squares have (almost) no cropland and are skipped.

4. What the figures show
------------------------
- Morocco's cropland is one dense belt (Gharb, Saiss, Chaouia, Doukkala, Tadla, Haouz); most blocks are
  almost full (dark). Utah's cropland is scattered along valleys and rivers; most blocks hold only a few
  cropland tiles (light), so per block fewer tiles are written.
- Because neighbouring Sentinel-2 tiles overlap by 9.8 km, nearly every 10.24 km block lies entirely inside
  one Sentinel-2 tile. For one date a block job therefore usually reads one file per band, two where tiles or
  orbits overlap.
- Each region spans two UTM zones (Morocco 29 and 30, Utah 11 and 12). Sentinel-2 tiles of the other zone are
  reprojected onto the region's grid (EPSG:32629 Morocco, EPSG:32612 Utah), as Earth Engine did for the
  training tiles.


5. Why these sizes
------------------
- Model tile 128 px: the size of the training tiles and the input of every model (a whole number of 16 px and
  8 px patches). Keeping it means mask, labels, images and maps line up without resampling.
- Block 10.24 km: one job of moderate size (about 1-2 GB in memory for one month of scenes). 5.12 km blocks
  would need 3.4 times more jobs and catalogue searches; 20.48 km blocks read more empty land and need 4 times
  more memory. 10.24 km is also close to the 0.1 degree (about 11 km) tiles used by TESSERA for its global
  embeddings.
