# Week 2026-W47 · pixel-wise RQ1 done, fine-tuning running

**In one line:** the study moved from parcels to pixels; the pixel-wise RQ1 experiments E01–E04 are done in both
regions (frozen GeoFMs clearly better in Utah, a tie in Morocco), and E05 (Prithvi frozen / fine-tuned / from
scratch with fewer labels) is running on the cluster.

  parcel, so the model can map the whole area; the parcel-wise experiments are
  [archived](../archive/index.md) ([D16](../decisions.md#d16-pixel-wise-classification-for-continuous-maps)).

  → [cropland mask](../phase1_data/cropland_mask.md), [pixel samples](../phase1_data/pixel_samples.md).

  [RQ1 · Experiment plan](../phase2_models/experiment_plan.md).

  fine-tuning, training from scratch), each with 10, 25, 50 and 100% of the training parcels; frozen, fine-tuned and
  from scratch are running on the cluster, LoRA follows
  → [E05](../experiments/E05_prithvi_strategies.md), [RQ2 · Fine-tuning](../phase2_models/fine_tuning.md).

## 2. Results

Cropland macro-F1 (irrigated, rainfed), out of fold; significance against the random forest:

| Model | Morocco | Utah |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| E01 random forest | 0.876 | 0.729 |
| E02 TempCNN | 0.886 | 0.721 |
| E03 U-TAE from scratch | 0.897 | **0.766** (significant) |
| E04 best frozen GeoFM | 0.900 (AgriFM) | **0.797** (TerraMind, significant) |

In Morocco all models lie within 0.876–0.900 and no difference is significant. In Utah the four frozen GeoFMs beat
the random forest by 0.055–0.068, and spatial context (U-TAE) adds +0.037.
