# Week 2026-W47 · pixel-wise RQ1 done, fine-tuning running

**In one line:** the study moved from parcels to pixels; the pixel-wise RQ1 experiments E01–E04 are done in both
regions (frozen GeoFMs clearly better in Utah, a tie in Morocco), and E05 (Prithvi frozen / fine-tuned / from
scratch with fewer labels) is running on the cluster.

## 1. Done this week
- **Pixel-wise classification** of every 10 m pixel (irrigated, rainfed, not cropland) instead of one label per
  parcel, so the model can map the whole area; the parcel-wise experiments are
  [archived](../archive/index.md) ([D16](../decisions.md#d16-pixel-wise-classification-for-continuous-maps)).
- **Cropland mask** (union of four products) and **pixel samples** for Morocco and Utah
  → [cropland mask](../phase1_data/cropland_mask.md), [pixel samples](../phase1_data/pixel_samples.md).
- **E01–E04 run in both regions:** random forest, TempCNN, U-TAE, and four frozen GeoFMs (Prithvi, Clay,
  TerraMind, AgriFM) with L-TAE + UPerNet → [experiments](../experiments/index.md),
  [RQ1 · Experiment plan](../phase2_models/experiment_plan.md).
- **E05 designed and started** (RQ2, Utah): **four strategies with Prithvi-EO-2.0** (frozen encoder, LoRA, full
  fine-tuning, training from scratch), each with 10, 25, 50 and 100% of the training parcels; frozen, fine-tuned and
  from scratch are running on the cluster, LoRA follows
  → [E05](../experiments/E05_prithvi_strategies.md), [RQ2 · Fine-tuning](../phase2_models/fine_tuning.md).
- **STAC download** of wall-to-wall Sentinel-2 composites for the maps: Utah 2023/24 at 17,278 of 18,472 tiles; a
  crash in 3 of 32 parts found and fixed (78 blocks to rerun).


## 2. Results

!!! warning "Withdrawn after the meeting"
    These 3-class results (irrigated, rainfed, not cropland) were withdrawn: the not-cropland labels came from the
    cropland products, not from the field. The experiments run again in two classes, Morocco first (section 5).

Cropland macro-F1 (irrigated, rainfed), out of fold; significance against the random forest:

| Model | Morocco | Utah |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| E01 random forest | 0.876 | 0.729 |
| E02 TempCNN | 0.886 | 0.721 |
| E03 U-TAE from scratch | 0.897 | **0.766** (significant) |
| E04 best frozen GeoFM | 0.900 (AgriFM) | **0.797** (TerraMind, significant) |

In Morocco all models lie within 0.876–0.900 and no difference is significant. In Utah the four frozen GeoFMs beat
the random forest by 0.055–0.068, and spatial context (U-TAE) adds +0.037.

## 5. Notes from the meeting
- **Question raised:** the third class "not cropland" has no field data; its labels come from the cropland products,
  so its loss and scores are not comparable with the surveyed classes.
- **Decision** ([D19](../decisions.md#d19-two-class-experiments-cropland-extent-in-the-mapping-phase)): the
  experiments compare models on the surveyed parcels only, in **two classes (irrigated, rainfed)**; no cropland mask
  in labels, samples or experiments. The cropland extent (union of products vs Dynamic World, etc.) is chosen later,
  in the mapping phase.
- **Consequence:** all pixel-wise results removed; E01–E05 rerun in two classes, **Morocco first**, then Utah; E05
  moves to Morocco. Code and experiment pages updated and tested on 20 November.
