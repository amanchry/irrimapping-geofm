    2026-11-05 Results that go into the thesis are produced as experiments (config per experiment, experiments/); two temporal-aggregation pipelines: concat + PCA 128 (E01, E02) and L-TAE (E04) Reproducibility; PANGAEA (Marsocci et al. 2024)
    2026-11-04 Validation = PASTIS rotation (3 folds train / next fold validation / 1 fold test); nested CV kept as sensitivity check (E01) Garnot & Landrieu 2021; one design for classifiers and neural networks

    2026-10-15 Foundation models compared: Clay, Prithvi-EO-2.0, TerraMind, AgriFM (AlphaEarth only a pipeline test, not in the thesis) Thesis proposal
    2026-10-15 Main comparison with frozen encoders + the same 4 classifiers; fine-tuning only as a secondary experiment Fair across models; Butsko et al. 2025, Marsocci et al. 2024
    2026-10-15 Time window = agricultural year 1 Sep - 31 Aug; growing season only (Nov-May) and calendar year as ablations Ministry reports season rainfall from 1 Sep; sowing ~Nov, harvest May-Jul (FAO GIEWS; USDA FAS 2017); summer is when only irrigated land stays green
    2026-10-15 Each parcel gets imagery from its own survey season (ag_season: 2023/24 or 2024/25); both years pooled in one dataset, periods aligned (p01 = September) Survey ran Dec 2023 - Jan 2025; dropping 2024/25 would lose 42% of rainfed parcels

    2026-10-15 Validation = nested spatial CV on 20 km blocks (5 folds); random CV only to show its optimism Roberts et al. 2017; Ploton et al. 2020; Kattenborn et al. 2022
    2026-10-15 Metrics: macro-F1 (main), per-class F1/precision/recall, balanced accuracy, PR-AUC, Brier, ECE; 95% CI by block bootstrap; inputs compared by paired differences + Holm Foody 2009; Pontius & Millones 2011 (no kappa)
    2026-10-15 Classifiers: logistic regression (linear probe), random forest, histogram gradient boosting, RBF SVM Alain & Bengio 2016; Breiman 2001; Gao et al. 2018; Elwan et al. 2022
    2026-10-15 Cloud mask: Cloud Score+ (cs_cdf >= 0.60) instead of QA60; scene filter raised to 60% cloud Pasquarella et al. 2023 (GEE dataset page: 0.50-0.65)
    2026-10-15 S1/S2 empty periods filled by linear interpolation in time per parcel (15-day file: 79% of parcels had a gap) Avoids constant median fill
    2026-10-15 Parcels shrunk by 10 m (one pixel) for S1/S2 feature extraction Removes mixed edge pixels
    2026-10-15 One shared chip store (64 x 64 px, 10 m, 12 months, 12 S2 bands + VV/VH) for all foundation models Same imagery for every model; one download
    2026-10-15 Parcel embedding = patches covering the parcel, weighted by the share inside the parcel Parcel-level labels
    2026-10-15 Clay: monthly chips one by one, 10 bands, Clay normalisation, week/lat-lon codes Clay v1.5 docs and code
    2026-10-15 Prithvi: 6 bands (B2 B3 B4 B8A B11 B12), 4 frames per pass, date + location; 10 m input (pretrained on 30 m HLS) Model card config.json
    2026-10-15 TerraMind: S2L2A (12 bands) + S1GRD, standardised with v1 pretraining stats, modalities averaged TerraTorch terramind_register.py
    2026-11-06 AgriFM: patch size 4 × 4 × 4 px of the pretrained patch embedding (stride 32 px) instead of the fine-tuning config's 4 × 2 × 2; weights mapped by name and checked on loading AgriFM.pth (S2 patch kernel 128 × 10 × 4 × 4 × 4); D12
    2026-10-15 AgriFM: 12 months at once, 10 bands, normalisation from its cropland config; loaded without mmcv (mmengine registries) AgriFM repo configs/cropland_mapping.py

    An independent code review (no data leakage found; draws identical across scripts; E02 and E03 inputs identical, checked) and a docs check led to these fixes: - Quick checks (QUICK_TEST, QUICK = True) write to experiments/<E>/results_quick/ and write no report; before, a quick run left oof_predictions.csv in the real folder and the real run would have been skipped. - Nested label-efficiency draws: one random order of the blocks per region, round and draw; a fraction takes its first blocks (10% ⊂ 25% ⊂ 50%), accepted if the first 10% hold ≥ 20 rainfed parcels. - Single-seed curve points: 03b and 03c save oof_per_seed.csv; the 100% point of a neural learning curve is the mean over its seeds scored one by one, like the single-seed label-efficiency runs. Training parcels per fraction are reported. - 04: the Holm family is the experiment's own runs only; new paired table differences_vs_earlier_experiments.csv (same input, this experiment's head vs the earlier experiments' heads: E03 vs E02, E04 vs E03 and E02); a borrowed experiment without runs or a missing baseline stops the script; development-mode paths built after the region override. - 05: reports every head (the filter dropped MLP / L-TAE / PSE-TAE); runs without a baseline (E05). - Runners: run_experiment.py and submit_slurm.py drop stray EXPERIMENT_* shell variables and set every run variable explicitly; submit_slurm.py submits a compare job only when the borrowed experiments are finished (or WAIT_FOR). - E04 config: HEAD: ltae (it would have run the MLP head); breakdown step added to E02–E05. - Report: sections for the head-vs-head table, spread over seeds, L-TAE attention per month, permutation importance, training curves and results by zone. - Docs: seeds (42, 1042, 2042), L-TAE time encoding, E04 implementation section, results-page contents (PCA explained variance dropped; crop-group breakdown and compute cost marked as later / by hand), status table.

    2026-11-04 (future phase: US data for spatial and temporal transfer — decision deferred)¶

    Discussed after reading IrrMap (Mandal et al. 2025, arXiv:2505.08273). Decision: complete Morocco first; come back to this later. - IrrMap itself is not usable as the study area. Cite IrrMap as related work and as a model for the dataset paper (Part 1). - Idea for a second phase: use the original field boundaries and labels behind IrrMap (state datasets), build chips from scratch with the same pipeline (labels -> 20 km block folds -> tile store -> chips -> embeddings -> same classifiers and validation), to test spatial and temporal transferability. - Why it is valuable: state data often covers many years over the same fields -> clean temporal transfer (train earlier years, test later years), which Morocco cannot offer (year and region overlap); a second continent tests whether GeoFMs transfer better than S1/S2 features (Morocco <-> US). - Conditions: (1) the source data must contain non-irrigated / dryland parcels (deal-breaker; e.g. Washington agricultural land-use data includes dryland wheat — to be checked state by state); (2) small scope: 1-2 states, 3-4 years, ~10-20k parcels; (3) only after Morocco is finished. - Pipeline changes needed: new label reader in ml_dataset_prep/01_prepare_labels.py; support other UTM zones in 03_build_tiles.py (now fixed to UTM 29N; western US spans zones 10-13); US season window via START_MONTH; optional fold by year in 02; a new transfer evaluation (train region/year A, test B). models_run/ unchanged. - Risks: administrative (noisier) labels; different crop systems (Morocco <-> US transfer may be poor for all models); ~4-8 extra weeks. - First step when resuming: one-day check of which state sources IrrMap used, whether they include dryland / non-irrigated fields, which years, and whether field boundaries are downloadable. - Fallback if the check fails: keep as future work; use GMIA-NEXT ground-truth points (tools/a1) or Tunisia as the external test.

    2026-11-04 (validation design: PASTIS scheme)¶

    • models_run/03_train_evaluate.py: new setting VALIDATION = "pastis" (default). Per round: 3 folds train, the next fold (k + 1) is the validation fold that chooses the hyperparameters, fold k is the test fold; the model is trained on the 3 training folds. Same scheme as PASTIS (Garnot & Landrieu 2021) and 03b_train_psetae.py, so classifiers and the neural-network baseline are trained, tuned and tested identically. VALIDATION = "nested" keeps the earlier design (inner spatial CV on 4 folds, refit on 4) for a sensitivity check; its runs get the suffix _nested, so run names of the default stay the ones 04_compare_models.py expects.
